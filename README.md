# Giuseppe Cimicata, PhD

**Protein Engineering | Structural Biology | Quantitative & Computational Biology**

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), working at the interface of experimental protein engineering, quantitative biophysics, and computational protein design.

My research focuses on understanding and engineering biomolecular systems through the integration of experimental measurements, structural information, quantitative modeling, and computational approaches.

My background spans protein design and engineering, structural biology, protein and nucleic-acid biochemistry, genome-editing systems, and scientific computing.

## Selected computational research

### Programmable Protein Switches

Python workflows developed for the quantitative characterization and thermodynamic modeling of programmable protein conformational switches.

The project integrates experimental titrations, nonlinear parameter estimation, mechanistic equilibrium modeling, prediction of two-dimensional switching landscapes, and quantitative model-to-experiment validation.

**Tools:** Python · NumPy · pandas · SciPy · Matplotlib

[View repository](LINK_TO_PROGRAMMABLE_PROTEIN_SWITCHES)

### Cas9 Sequence Engineering

Python tools developed during a Cas9 protein-engineering project to connect computational variant selection with experimentally usable sequence designs.

The workflow includes candidate selection, protein-sequence extraction and comparison, reconstruction of engineered protein regions, and generation of corresponding DNA construct sequences.

**Tools:** Python · pandas · NumPy · Matplotlib · openpyxl

[View repository](https://github.com/giuseppecimicata-ux/Cas9-sequence-engineering)
